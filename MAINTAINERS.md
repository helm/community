# Helm Maintainers

The Helm maintainers are the active members of the groups documented in
[maintainer-groups.yaml](maintainer-groups.yaml), as defined in the [Helm governance](governance/governance.md).
The `helm/community` repository is administered by Helm Admins on behalf of the Helm maintainers.
