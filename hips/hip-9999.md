## Abstract

Establish a single level of Helm maintainership and delegate administrative and security responsibilities to teams of
active maintainers.

## Motivation

Given the size of the Helm maintainer community, a separate elected tier of Org maintainers introduces unnecessary
organizational overhead and complicates access to shared project resources.

## Rationale

All active maintainers should participate equally in project-wide decisions while retaining autonomy within their
maintainer groups. Administrative responsibilities do not require a separate level of governance authority.
This preserves the distributed ownership model defined in [HIP-0007](hip-0007.md).

## Specification

- Remove the Org maintainer role and its election process.
- Give all active maintainers equal project-wide decision-making authority, with one vote per person.
- Retain maintainer groups, existing voting thresholds, and scoped technical decision-making.
- Apply the existing employer diversity requirement to the collective maintainer body.
- Establish Helm Admins and the Security Team as delegated teams composed of active maintainers, without additional
  governance authority.
- Have Helm Admins administer `helm/community` and verify required governance approvals.
- Provide all maintainers with access to shared project resources.

No existing active maintainer loses maintainership in this transition.

## Reference implementation

Update the [governance documents](../governance/) and associated membership records and procedures.
Archive all repositories currently owned by Org maintainers except `helm/community` before merging the governance changes.
Approval must follow the governance rules in effect before this proposal is adopted.
