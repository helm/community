# Helm Governance

The Helm governance details areas such as:

* Maintainer responsibilities
* How maintainers are organized into maintainer groups and delegated teams
* How maintainer group and delegated team members are selected
* Decision making
* Code of Conduct handling

For the full detail on the Helm governance see the document [governance.md](governance.md).

Helm has a single level of maintainer. The maintainer groups, their repositories, and where each one records its members are listed in [maintainer-groups.yaml](../maintainer-groups.yaml).

Delegated team membership records are linked from [MAINTAINERS.md](../MAINTAINERS.md).
